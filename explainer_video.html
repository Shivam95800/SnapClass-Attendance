03 / COMPUTER VISION PIPELINE

Multi-Face Recognition & Anti-Spoofing

Detects dozens of students simultaneously in a single classroom wide-angle photograph and matches them against pre-registered vector embeddings.

📐
68-Point Landmarks
dlib frontal face detector locates contours, jawline, eye coordinates, and mouth landmarks.
🧬
128D Face Descriptors
Deep ResNet generates 128-dimensional embedding vectors for high-precision Euclidean distance matching.
👁️
Eye-Blink Liveness (EAR)
Calculates Eye Aspect Ratio dynamics across frames to confirm a live student and block photo spoofs.
04 / VOICE BIOMETRICS PIPELINE

Voiceprint AI & Anti-Replay Challenge

For audio roll-call, students speak aloud. The pipeline extracts acoustic characteristics and ensures liveness using speech recognition.

Active Session Challenge:
🎙️ "Falcon 7 4 2"

Randomly generated tokens ensure recorded voice clips cannot be replayed for proxy attendance. Google STT verifies the phrase.

🔊
Resemblyzer & Librosa
Splits classroom audio streams at silence thresholds, extracts 256D normalized voiceprints, and identifies speakers via Cosine Similarity.
05 / ENROLLMENT & SECURITY

Dynamic QR Codes with Session TTL

Teachers generate course join QR codes and links. Sessions expire automatically after configured durations (15m, 30m, 1h) to prevent link leakages.

📲
One-Click Auto-Enroll
Students scan the QR code to be instantly enrolled into the subject via URL query parameters.
⏳
Configurable TTL
Session tokens in Firestore automatically invalidate after time limits expire.
🔒
Bcrypt & Zero Leaks
Teacher passwords are cryptographically salted; Firebase credentials stay isolated in secrets.
